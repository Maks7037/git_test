Top 10 Famous Places in Switzerland

A brief overview of the best locations

1. Matterhorn

Iconic 4,478m pyramid-shaped mountain peak in Zermatt.

2. Jungfraujoch

Europe’s highest railway station with views of the Aletsch Glacier.

3. Lauterbrunnen Valley

Scenic Alpine valley famous for its cliffs and 72 waterfalls.

4. Chillon Castle

Historic medieval fortress located on Lake Geneva.

5. Kapellbrücke

Europe’s oldest covered wooden bridge in Lucerne.

6. Rhine Falls

Europe’s largest plain waterfall located near Schaffhausen.

7. Bern Old Town

UNESCO heritage center with medieval arcades and the Zytglogge clock tower.

8. Lake Geneva

Famous lake with the iconic 140-meter Jet d'Eau fountain.

9. Glacier Express

Legendary panoramic train routes through the Alps.

10. Lindt Home of Chocolate

Interactive chocolate museum near Zurich with a giant chocolate fountain.
